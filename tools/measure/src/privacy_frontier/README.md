# Privacy frontier and provenance

provenance.rs checks bounded declared study/policy/session/holdout relationships; binary.rs computes empirical binary-hypothesis diagnostics after the parent consistency gate.

### Contracts and limits

Hashes bind bytes and pair keys align declared trials; neither proves capture origin, statistical independence or a population law. Reports retain whole-flow, AWGN and population guarantees as unestablished when premises are absent. Synthetic tests are schema/diagnostic controls only.

### Entry points and maintenance

Used by ../privacy_frontier.rs. Keep training/evaluation separation, fixed alphabet/bin assumptions and unverified-provenance behavior explicit.
