# Code of Conduct

AEGIS contributors should be able to participate without harassment. This applies
to project issues, reviews, discussions and project-organized collaboration,
including private channels and interactions with automated assistants.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, use clear technical criticism and allow room for correction.
- Protect private identity, credentials, operational data and unpublished reports.
- Credit contributions accurately; distinguish measured results from claims.
- Avoid personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment,
  deliberate intimidation, doxxing and repeated unwanted contact.
- Follow agreed access boundaries and stop requests; do not retaliate against
  someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement readiness

**A confidential enforcement contact and an independent escalation reviewer have
not yet been appointed.** The repository owner must appoint consenting people
and a private reporting channel before public community intake. Do not post a
private incident, identity or allegation in a public issue. Existing approved
contributors can request a confidential channel from the owner who granted
access; this does not promise an independent or monitored reporting service.

The intended process is to acknowledge a report, limit access to necessary
reviewers, let affected people respond where safe, and record the decision and
appeal route. Responses may include a correction request, warning, temporary
participation restriction or removal of project access, proportionate to the
conduct and risk. The owner must establish who decides, how conflicts of
interest are handled and who hears appeals. These procedures are not claimed
operational until that appointment is recorded.

Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); personal safety and
harassment reports should not include exploit or enrollment material.
